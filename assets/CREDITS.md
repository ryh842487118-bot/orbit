Downloaded 2026-09-06. The image bytes are unchanged; local filenames were simplified.

Required attribution: credit Solar System Scope, link to the source and CC BY 4.0 license, and identify any future modifications. Do not imply endorsement or impose additional legal/technical restrictions on the textures.

Source notes: textures are based on NASA elevation and imagery data. Colors have been adjusted; unmapped areas may contain matching fictional terrain. These are visual reconstruction maps rather than unmodified spacecraft photographs.

The standard-resolution sphere maps are 2048 × 1024 JPEG. Saturn rings are a 2048 × 125 PNG strip with alpha. Cloud JPEG has no alpha channel; its brightness can serve as a cloud opacity map. The additional Earth day/night maps listed below are 8192 × 4096 JPEG, downloaded unchanged from the same official source on 2026-09-06 UTC (2026-09-07 in Asia/Shanghai).
